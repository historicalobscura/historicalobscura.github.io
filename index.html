yt-auto

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel Historical Obscura.

What it does

yt-auto runs on the channel owner's own computer. It produces original 3D-animated science videos ("What If?" space and science scenarios) and uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload those videos to the owner's own channel and schedule their publication,
  • set titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails,
  • read the channel's own analytics to plan future videos.

yt-auto is not offered to the public. It acts only on the channel that authorized it.
